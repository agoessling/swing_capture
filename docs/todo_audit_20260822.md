## Physical and human dependencies

These are not software-complete merely because deterministic infrastructure exists:

- off-nominal golfer behavior needs representative reviewed field evidence;
- audio detector selection needs at least two fully reviewed phone/view-swapped, negative-rich
  holdout sessions with the version-2 category repetition;
- five-minute/30-minute runs require explicit user authorization under the HIL agreement;
- receiver delivery, device-protected boot-marker persistence, and reboot/unlock/foreground-launch
  recovery require the explicitly approved two-phone OS-reboot ceremony;
- prototype release qualification requires a reviewed exact-APK phone-browser gate artifact;
- absolute acoustic calibration requires a trustworthy physical reference; and
- visual design approval remains a human acceptance point after reproducible screenshots and
  accessibility tests pass.
